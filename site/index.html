How it works

Three steps, and the money is accounted for at every one.

01

A brand funds a campaign

The brand sets a commission rate and deposits the budget up front. It sits in a campaign balance before a single creator is invited.

02

Creators share a tracked link

Approved creators get their own link for each product. Every click is counted once, and every order that comes from it is matched back.

03

Earnings are paid out

Commission is calculated on the order, held through the brand's return window, then paid into your own South African bank account.

Who it's for

Built for both sides of the deal.

For creators

  • Earn a percentage of every confirmed sale you drive.
  • See your clicks, conversions and earnings as they happen.
  • Paid into your own bank account, in rands.
  • No minimum follower count — your results speak.

For brands

  • Pay for sales, not for reach or guesswork.
  • Connect your store and orders are attributed automatically.
  • Set your own commission rate and return window.
  • See exactly which creator drove which order.

Why the money is held first. Affiliate programmes usually ask creators to do the work and trust that payment arrives. COVO turns that around: a brand's budget is deposited before creators are invited, and a campaign cannot promise more commission than it holds. If the balance runs short, earnings are recorded and paid as soon as it is topped up — never quietly dropped.
